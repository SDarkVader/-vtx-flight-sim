# Vaderland 3D Rotating Cylindrical Office & Canonical Characters

This package contains the high-fidelity 3D rotating multi-department office cylinder environment and canonical character models for **Raymond Winston** and **Arthur Finch**, built to match the target reference artwork.

---

## 📦 Package Contents

### 1. Master 3D Environment
* **`Vaderland_RotatingOffice_Full3D_v1.blend`**:
  * Seamless rotating habitat cylinder ($R = 15\text{ m}, L = 60\text{ m}$) with continuous floor deck.
  * 12 distinct departments flush-mounted to the interior curved deck.
  * Central open airspace ($R \in [0, 11]\text{ m}$) with autonomous flying quad-drones.
  * Active circular launchpad turntable with fighter interceptor.
  * Master rotation animation keyed over 360 frames (linear cyclic loop). Press **Spacebar** in Blender to rotate the habitat around the longitudinal axis.
  * Configured directly in **Material Preview (`MATERIAL`)** mode with Scene Lights and Scene World enabled for immediate full-color visual rendering upon opening.

### 2. Standalone Canonical Characters
* **Raymond Winston (Management):**
  * `Raymond_Winston_Canonical_v1.blend`
  * `Raymond_Winston_Canonical_v1.glb`
  * Features: Bowler hat with gold taxi emblem, loose red tartan necktie, gold "RAYMOND" nameplate, cyan arched eyes `^ ^`, glowing ear cuffs, 17-bone biped rig.
* **Arthur Finch (Compliance & Records):**
  * `Arthur_Finch_Canonical_v1.blend`
  * `Arthur_Finch_Canonical_v1.glb`
  * Features: Chamfered CRT monitor head, glowing amber eyes `_ _`, pencil behind ear, charcoal knitted vest with gold buttons, compliance badge, 17-bone biped rig.

### 3. Textures & References
* **`vaderland_canonical_unwrapped_map.jpg`**: Seamless unwrapped cylindrical texture map.
* **`targets/`**: Canonical design references for character fidelity and cylindrical layout.
* **`proofs/`**: Rendered visual proofs covering all 13 primary cameras and department views.
* **`scripts/`**: Procedural Blender Python generation and verification scripts.
* **`VADERLAND_FULL3D_CYLINDRICAL_DELIVERY.md`**: Comprehensive technical delivery report.
