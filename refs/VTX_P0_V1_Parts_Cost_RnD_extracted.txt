 Why weight targets are not yet released
 The 75 kg frame allocation uses an illustrative 40 kg central ring plus four internally stiffened arms of
 approximately 8.76 kg each. Shortening these arms requires the central ring to accept the transferred loads.
 Local panel buckling may invalidate the selected 3.5 mm skins without verified transverse stiffness and webs.
 The 88 kg cabin includes a speculative 14 kg increment over the earlier monolithic-glass concept to allow
 laminated glazing.

 07 / Principal engineering uncertainties
   ID       RISK                                SPECIFIC IMPACT

   U01      Four-thrust-channel control loss    The simplified allocation loses a degree of independent control after one rotor
                                                fails; backup torque/authority not demonstrated.

   U02      Common turbine failure              All four rotor branches share one prime mover; no power-out continued-hover
                                                capability in P0.

   U03      Drive torque & dynamics             Gear reduction, shaft critical speeds and torsional vibration under cyclic rotor
                                                loading unverified.

   U04      Duct aero / structural distortion   Blade tip clearance, edgewise-flow losses, rotor wake interference and
                                                containment not yet tested.

   U05      Cabin impact and gimbal integrity   Laminated glazing and composite panel impact/load allowables not
                                                substantiated.

   U06      Mass budget / CG                    No solid CAD or weighed subassembly; fore-aft fuel/crew balance and mass
                                                moments of inertia unknown.

   U07      SFC & power integration             Reference 0.4 kg/kWh independent of engine power and altitude is unvalidated;
                                                rated power not constant.

   U08      Test configuration maturity         Drawings are parametric concept views, not fabrication or toleranced
                                                machining drawings.




 Development spending priorities
 Do not first optimise only cost per kilogram. Priority 1 is a testable rotor/thrust/engine envelope; priority 2 a
 verified stiff structural load path; priority 3 control authority with asymmetric loads and credible
 propulsion-failure response; priority 4 aerodynamic optimisation of duct lip, blade and stator; priority 5
 component-level production design.


 Budget sensitivity
 Changing fuel from GBP 1.45/L to GBP 1.82/L (both published UK airfield examples, [S10-S11]) alters P0-V1
 calculated 27 km mission fuel cost from approximately GBP 45.58 to GBP 57.22 at the nominal assumed
 engine efficiency. That fuel variation is small compared with airframe development costs.
