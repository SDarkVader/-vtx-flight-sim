 baseline; component masses, aerodynamic coefficients, engine-specific fuel consumption,
 crashworthiness and flight-control performance are not substantiated by qualification tests.

 Control definition: P0-V1 is a single-turbine, mechanically distributed, four-ducted-rotor VTOL with a two-axis
 gimballed two-seat spherical cabin. It is a controlled analytical prototype concept, not a certified, validated or
 flight-proven aircraft.

 Design materials and assemblies
 Cabin: 35% nominal glazed surface area, laminated aviation glazing allowance, CFRP sandwich opaque
 structure and qualified flame-resistant internal trim. Main frame: carbon-composite load ring and stiffened box
 supports; metallic inserts at highly concentrated load paths. Fan modules: composite blades/duct plus metallic
 bearing and gearing elements. These statements identify candidates, not released laminate schedules.

 04 / Formulae and numerical flight model
 Primary hover-equivalent induced shaft power (actuator-disk approximation):

 P_hover = (m g / η) × sqrt[(m g) / (2 ρ A)]

 Turn reference: 27.0 km at 100.0 km/h (972 s) is modelled as an equivalent continuous-radius turn, plus 80 s
 of hover/transition power; total 1052 s, 17 minutes 32 seconds. This is a test-energy benchmark rather than a
 complete time-resolved control simulation.

 Drag force: D = 0.5 ρ V² C_D A; baseline assumed C_D A = 1.65 m². Net turn thrust vector magnitude: T =
 sqrt[(m g)² + (m V²/R)² + D²]. Forward-flight shaft power is approximated as induced-power-equivalent for T
 plus D V / η_prop. This is not a blade-element-momentum or edgewise duct-performance computation.

   MODEL INPUT                                                 NOMINAL

   Rotor aerodynamic efficiency η                              0.72 (unverified aggregate)

   Forward propulsive efficiency η_prop                        0.75 (unverified)

   Engine specific fuel consumption                            0.400 kg/kWh (unverified part-load placeholder)

   Fuel density                                                0.800 kg/L (planning assumption)

   Fuel pump price                                             GBP 1.45/L, Brighton September 2026 [S10]

   Main turn reference radius                                  500 m; stress-turn radius 200 m

 Composite arm reference section
 Illustrative 1.50 m cantilever from ring to rotor hub; 340 × 120 mm CFRP box section, 3.5 mm skins, two
 internal webs. At illustrative 1.5g thrust and 900 Nm torque the simplified beam study predicted ~1.4 mm tip
 deflection, ~0.33° twist and ~43.9 Hz bending mode. These values are NOT structural allowables; joint
 compliance, laminate coupling, web local buckling and load concentrations were excluded.

 07 / Flight control and gimballed cabin
   FUNCTION                                 CONCEPT CONTROL / SENSOR PATH

   Vehicle attitude                         IMU feedback commands four independent collective-pitch actuators on common-speed
                                            shaft-driven fans; vehicle roll/pitch/yaw

   Translational manoeuvring                Controlled vehicle tilt and thrust magnitude; fixed planar rotors cannot independently vector
                                            thrust

   Cabin coordination                       Two-axis pitch/roll gimbal; bank to resultant acceleration in turns rather than always remain
                                            horizontal

   Occupant asymmetry                       Measured/estimated CG offset, preflight trim and bounded control corrections

   Sensor suite                             Independent IMUs, rotor speed/torque measurement, air data, fuel, vibration, bus power,
                                            gimbal position

   Power interruption                       Engine-out and driveline faults are unresolved critical hazards; first programme uses
                                            uncrewed tethered testing

   Rotor-out allocation                     Four nonnegative variable-pitch lift channels cannot generally preserve all 4 thrust/moment
                                            DOF after one fan or drive branch fails

 Programme hypothesis to test
 The design may complete a 27 km 100 km/h equivalent mission for approximately 25.2 kg Jet A-1 on the
 nominal low-order model, with adequate nominal turbine takeoff power. This is a feasibility hypothesis only.
 Before a human-occupied or untethered prototype can be justified, the single-engine and loss-of-one-fan
 control risks must be resolved or the test campaign redesigned to contain their consequences.
