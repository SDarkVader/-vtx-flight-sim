# Phantom A9: MuJoCo integration handover (wing cassette, control surfaces, canards)

**Machine-readable sources:**

- `evidence/phantom/a9_handover.json`: bodies, mass properties, joints, sites, aero surfaces and control limits.
- `evidence/phantom/a9_mujoco_fragment.xml`: a generated MJCF subtree.
- `evidence/phantom/a9_mujoco_check.json`: the load-and-drive check.

All are regenerated by `tools/phantom/export_handover.py` and `tools/phantom/mujoco_articulation_check.py` from the A9 `.blend`.

**Status:** articulation, masses and inertias are DERIVED (geometry plus declared masses) or SYNTHETIC. Aerodynamic data are screening values from `engineering/phantom/wing_loads.py`. **Not** included:

- the chassis (A8 hull, tub, occupants, powertrain, energy);
- structural compliance;
- aerodynamic forces;
- contact geometry.

The A8 wheel-pod and leg masses in the JSON are marked `A8 PLACEHOLDER` (solid CAD volume × CFRP density). They are not estimates.

## Frame

The Blender model frame is X forward, Z up, right-handed, in metres. Use the same axes for the MuJoCo chassis body. A8 objects named `RIGHT` sit at **+Y**, which is the vehicle's port side in this right-handed frame. A9 keeps the A8 names (`_R` = +Y, `_L` = −Y) and records `side_y_sign`. Settle the naming before any pilot-facing labels are wired.

The MuJoCo check (`a9_mujoco_check.json`) drives every A9 joint to both range limits under gravity, using position actuators capped as above with rate-ramped targets and `integrator="implicitfast"`. **All 20 joints reach both limits.** The peak efforts there are gravity and inertia only (e.g. slide 177 N, pins 29 N). **Aerodynamic hinge moments and wing loads are not in that check** and must come from the surface-force model.

## Aerodynamic surfaces (screening values; replace with CFD/polars)

- **Wing:** S_ref 4.06 m², span 5.19 m, MAC 0.872 m, aerodynamic centre at x = −0.806 m (MAC quarter chord). The right wing's chord plane sits at z ≈ 1.00 m and the left at 0.88 m: a **0.12 m stagger**, so model each wing's force at its own height. Each exposed panel is 0.756 m². Section is NACA 2411 → 2410 (stand-in). CLα ≈ 4.0 /rad (M 0.15), CLmax 1.2 clean / 1.6 flapped, cm0 −0.04.
- **Ailerons:** η 0.55–0.95, 0.067 m² each, c_f/c 0.28, τ 0.64. **Flaps:** η 0.05–0.50, 0.114 m² each. Hinge coefficients C_hδ −0.70 and C_hα −0.35 per rad (±30%).
- **Canards:** 0.278 m² each, all-moving, pivot at 25% MAC (x 1.80), span |y| 1.26–1.84 at z 0.95, NACA 0009, CLα 3.0 /rad (SYNTHETIC).
- Evaluate local airflow at each surface using v + ω × r, and apply force at the surface's aerodynamic centre, per `SYSTEMS_CONTRACT.md`. Surfaces produce no lift with the wings stowed (slide < 0), and no aileron or flap moment unless the wing is locked.

## Integration steps

1. Wrap `a9_mujoco_fragment.xml` inside the Phantom chassis body. Add the A8 pods and legs and the chassis inertia from a mass ledger, which doesn't exist yet.
2. Attach aerodynamic sites at the surface aerodynamic centres. Add the force model (separate module, per `SYNTHETIC_COMPONENTS.md`).
3. Gate the slide, lock and hatch commands through the interlock sequence. Log requested vs achieved state and the lock-sensor state.
4. Add the failure cases from `SYNTHETIC_COMPONENTS.md`: hatch jam, partial slide, one pin not home (a locked wing must be shown as **not** locked), surface hard-over, and loss of actuator bus.
