## What worked (with evidence)
- **Surfaces already move in the live client.** `plant.visual_geometry()` tags each surface mesh with its MuJoCo body, `body_poses` carries that body's pose, and `public/vtx-env/harbour.js` (`applyBoatPoses`) applies it to the GLB node. This was checked by reading the code paths, not by running the live client.
- **Hinge points match the model.** `docs/ricochet/complete_model/export_rig.py` reads hinges, axes and ranges from the compiled MuJoCo model. The GLB node origins match the MuJoCo body origins to 4 µm.
- **Surfaces follow their commands at rest.** Bench sweep: 20 segments, 733 frames, still water, nothing live. Aileron, elevator, rudder and reverse buckets each reached the commanded angle within 0.01°, with the documented signs.
- **Interference at full travel.** Ailerons, elevator and reverse buckets: no vertex inside the neighbouring fixed mesh. Rudder: see failure 3.
- **Cockpit study (earlier today, 30 m/s):** the nine enclosure cases are in `scratch/cockpit_study/cockpit_study.json`. Craft CdA ranged 0.506 to 0.554 m² against 0.518 m² for the current bubble. The visual choice is nearly drag-neutral.

## What failed or is wrong (read this first)

1. **CRITICAL: the ski model adds energy at rest.** This blocks every profile performance result.
   - Repro: `docs/ricochet/complete_model/ski_collapse_probe.py <worktree> <out.json> full`. Log: `scratch/complete_model/ski_collapse_probe_full.json`.
   - Throttle 0, still water. After the surface sweeps and during the ski-collective hold, the airframe climbs from z 5.95 m to about 9.3 m against a water level of 7.0 m, and pitches up to about 21°.
   - The ski regime reads "planing" at near-zero forward speed.
   - Ski hinge moments reach about −4,200 N·m. The Exlar's peak is 1,416 N·m.
   - The skis are driven to about −4.6°, past their −3° stop.
   - Isolated ski collective and differential runs (no prior sweeps) hold steady at 12° and 6°, so the fault appears only after the earlier surface moves.
   - Not yet explained: in the isolated run the craft also settles from 6.44 m to about 5.47 m over 7 s with pitch to −3°. This could be correct ski loading or the same fault. Do not trust it until it is checked.
   - Suspects, not yet tested: `real_components.ski_hydro` planing and added-mass terms at u ≈ 0, and where the wrench is applied.

2. **The ski differential holds miss their targets in the sweep.** Because of failure 1, the sweep reports end errors of about 16° on the "ski differential +4" segment. Those numbers are not valid. Re-export after the fix.

3. **The rudder has no hinge cove.** The rudder's leading edge sits inside the tail fin at every angle (18 to 19 of 416 vertices within 4.5 cm of the hinge). Either the fin needs a cut-out or the rudder geometry must change. This is a design choice for you.

4. **The skis are not visible.** They exist in the physics (`body_poses`) but have no mesh in `visual_geometry()` and no node in the GLB. The live client cannot show them yet.

5. **The current bubble does not fit an average pilot.** Head clearance −0.26 m and shoulder clearance −0.06 m. Every enclosure that fits is taller than the current bubble, 0.70 to 0.99 m above the sill. Pilot dimensions are provisional, not yet checked against the ANSUR II report.

6. **Pilot CG does not match the load case.** The pilot CG sits at about (1.17 to 1.30, 1.11 to 1.16). The load case uses (1.30, 0.80). That puts the craft CG about 3 cm higher. Not reconciled.

   - Labels overlapped the next row and reflected off the wings. Relaid with metre drops.
   - Skis were missing from renders. Code added (`add_skis`), but the renders have not been redone.
   - I picked a design direction when you had asked me not to. Now only neutral options are presented, and the choice is yours.

## Follow-up verification & resolutions (Worktree `ricochet_control_surfaces`)
1. **Blocker #1 ("ski energy at rest") diagnosed & fixed (`src/vtx/water/shallow_water.py`):**
   - Root cause was missing reflective solid-wall hydrostatic momentum fluxes ($\frac{1}{2}gh^2$) on the 4 outer boundaries of `ShallowWaterSolver._advance_substep()`, which on wet-wall basins (`harbour_world()`) piled water to $h = 70.27\text{ m}$ at the walls and drained the interior by $1\text{–}2\text{ m}$ (also causing the $-0.97\text{ m}$ isolated-run drop and $-1.7\text{ m}$ `free_run_probe.json` drop).
   - Fixed with reflective ghost-cell wall fluxes (`max_swe_h_err_m = 0.0`, pulse reflection relative mass error $= 1.42 \times 10^{-16}$, rest bench sweep heave $z \in [6.434, 6.440]\text{ m}$, max rest ski hinge moment $18.85\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$).
2. **MuJoCo ski, servo & rudder cove calibration (`src/vtx/ricochet/real_components.py`, `plant.py`, `concept_a_builder.py`):**
   - Smooth $C^1$ `dof_armature` immersion ramp + Wagner water-entry slamming dissipation $-\max(\dot{I}_a, 0)\dot{q}$, tightened `solreflimit="0.005 1"` / `solimplimit="0.995 0.999 0.0002"`, regularized submerged CP, centroidal heave drag + 6-strip chordwise pitch-rate damping, smooth plate buoyancy, profile-gated Raymer `aero_buildup` drag, and profile-gated `rudder_cove_clear=True` (0 interfering vertices at $\pm 30^\circ$).
   - Wired across all 4 simulator layers (`plant.py`, `harbour_fleet.py`, `harbour_session.py`, `ricochet/live.py`, `public/vtx-env/harbour.js`) while keeping legacy Concept A (`profile=None`) and `public/assets/ricochet-concept-a.glb` 100% byte-identical.
