## Scope and version notes

- The 3 November Phantom campaign used the A9 cassette even when the historic variant key was `phantom` or `phantom-fx`. The later A10 keys reused those names. The cassette revision in each run's metadata controls the label here.
- The two 4 November mobile sorties are from the retained Vercel Sandbox and the `work/a10-airport-cockpit` branch. Their full compressed event logs are in the local PC archive and remain on that branch; no new raw logs are published by this index.
- Google Drive run folders found in the 4 November 2026 search were historical mirrors through 3 November. No additional 4 November run folder was found there; contents were not byte-compared.
- Blendstra's `VTX-A A10`, `A10 Phantom`, and `VTX Phantom A10 — Cassette model` are separate 3D assets, not flight records.
- A10 flat-strip plant tests are synthetic bench trials and have separate identifiers. See [A10 plant bench index](FLIGHT_BENCH_INDEX.md).
