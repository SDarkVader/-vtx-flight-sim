# Arbitrary-location map missions

The proprietor chooses any **valid** departure position and destination from the current test map, rather than preselected pads or scripted routes. Validate terrain support, stability and footprint, rotor/obstacle clearance and physically permissible landing area, with clear reasons for refusal.

The aircraft begins at rest on its gear at the selected departure. The **learning pilot** receives a destination and permitted sensor/map observations, then attempts takeoff, navigation and landing using the real modelled actuators and forces. It may fail at any phase. No teleportation, trajectory override, hidden lift, forced stabilisation, environment peeking or scripted successful touchdown.

Real ground reaction, impacts, loss of control, energy limits and rotor constraints determine what happens. Touchdown is not success unless supported and settled. Record attempted routes, observations, pilot actions, learning updates, flight/impact telemetry and physical outcomes for replay.

The exact same mission interface must work when the proprietor later supplies the unseen Stage 2 environment; do not grant advance knowledge of that world.
