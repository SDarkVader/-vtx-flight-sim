# Fan-housing full-rotation screen

The operator initially confirmed an end-over-end interpretation, then clarified that the housings raise by at most 20° and require full articulation rather than flipping. The intended motion is now understood as a 360° direction of tilt within a 20° envelope; the low point moves around the ring. This report preserves a separate, exploratory end-over-end sweep screen rather than claiming that the operator requests a flip. The screen uses the existing modeled nacelle pivot (for F1, approximately [2.60, 0, 2.03] m; the nominal rotor shaft center is 0.28 m above it). The blue-marked illustration is retained in `evidence/user-observations/fan-articulation/`.

`tools/check_full_rotation.py` screens the annular housing meshes in the R5 derived Blender geometry against the stationary airframe and cabin. At 30° increments about each of two candidate pivot axes, 32 of 96 sampled poses intersect existing geometry. The affected parts include the upper outer compression ring, nacelle braces, frame members and routed digital buses. The affected set contains 85 named parts: 69 structural parts and 16 bus components, with approximately 68.58 kg allocated in the provisional ledger. Their assigned mass is an estimate, not a measured redesign mass. These are geometric intersections, not an analysis of structural loads or proof of what a redesigned aircraft can do.

A placement sensitivity translated each housing and its pivot upward together, without moving or redesigning the rest of the aircraft. Raising them 0.5 m or 1.0 m left 28 of 96 sampled poses intersecting the stationary frame. Raising them 1.5 m or 2.0 m produced no housing/frame triangle intersections at those sampled poses. This is a candidate direction for investigation, not a complete redesign: bearings, shaft and power transmission, supports, other moving fans, actuators, clearance between samples, mass/CG, load paths, strength and occupant safety remain unassessed. The existing fan joints and live simulation have not been silently expanded or allowed to pass through the frame.

Reproduce with `blender -b assets/derived/p0-v4-live.blend --python tools/check_full_rotation.py`. Detailed pose results are in `evidence/full_rotation_clearance.json`. The Blender file is a linked library and reports an empty scene on loading, but its mesh datablocks are loaded and used by the script. The test checks triangle intersections only; containment and tolerances are not covered.

The operator identified the existing top joint as the intended pivot and subsequently clarified a 20° maximum housing tilt. The end-over-end intersections remain relevant only if that alternative is reconsidered. The desired low-point sweep needs a physical 20° combined-tilt constraint, a 360° direction command, actuator feedback and a complete swept-clearance assessment. No continuous-rotation bearing or end-over-end drive is assumed.
